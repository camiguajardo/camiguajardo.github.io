Branding


I’ve had the opportunity to work on branding at many different levels, which has given me a solid understanding of how each element contributes to building a strong visual identity. My experience ranges from creating logos, color palettes, and graphic systems for companies and organizations with diverse needs, to developing full brand guidelines from scratch.

One of my most enriching challenges was creating the complete visual identity and brand manual for a new brand launched by a major company in the beauty industry, a process that required deep conceptual thinking and strategic alignment.



Graphic identity design for a new digital platform for job searches specifically in the construction industry. 
This included a color palette that fits the context, a modern logo to reflect the platform’s digital nature while diffirenciating itself from similar companies, and an isotype designed for using in social media reductions.


Redesign of the visual identity for the Latin American Organization of HIV-Positive Women. This involved creating a new logo and a cleaner and more representative color palette: red symbolizes HIV awareness, purple represents the feminine identity, and the letter "p" in the logo and icon is designed to double as the red ribbon, main symbol for HIV awareness.



Freelance project for a university organization proposing an open space for students and professors to engage in open conversations, discussing changes, projects, and improvements to university life, with educational and psychological support. The logo's icon resembles a Lego block, symbolizing the step-by-step building process that each individual would contribute to the project.



Personal project for the creation of a children’s board game designed to educate about gender and inclusion. The visual identity, logo, color palette, and icon were inspired by playful and eye-catching elements, embracing a childlike, utopian fantasy of a society free from discrimination. The design appears as if crafted from paper, like a handmade project assembled piece by piece by children, just like the future of our society.
