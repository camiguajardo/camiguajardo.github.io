Hi! I'm Camila, but you can call me Cami


When I was 8, I designed a logo to encourage my grandmother to offer sewing lessons (spoiler: she never did, but she still keeps my creation to this day), and by 10, I had a small business beautifying presentations for my mom’s friends at work. I didn’t even know the concepts of “advertising” or “graphic design”, but I just knew this is what I wanted to do. I feel naturally driven to it and passionate for the way visuals have the power to evoke emotion and ultimately drive real change.

I’m an advertising professional with a minor in Social Sciences and a Gender Studies diploma, specializing in art direction with three years of experience. 

As part of creative teams in advertising agencies and in-house companies, I’ve had the chance to lead art direction for various brands in the food, automotive, beauty, travel/tourism and agrotech sectors. While contributing to the creative process behind ideas, campaigns, and concepts, my work spans digital and print graphic design, motion graphics, artificial intelligence image and video generarion, art direction for productions of all scales, and video editing for content creation.
